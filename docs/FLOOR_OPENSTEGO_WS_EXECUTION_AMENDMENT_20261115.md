phase closes unavailable at its 600-second watchdog, before any real model fit.
Preserve that parent's sources, freeze, observations and failure without relaunch.

its preparation, feature phase and diagnosis/overhead, leaving at most 850.
It is not a fresh budget or a retry of any actual fitted classifier.

compute unchanged deterministic descriptors, in the original ordered sequence.
Each uses one BLAS/OpenMP thread. No source image, feature definition, model,
role, seed, threshold policy or test access changes. The original unfinished
in-memory feature list has no persisted cache to reuse. A fresh source-bound
completion uses an exclusive new marker and may fit the ten models only once.

with the same four-GiB cap and process-group termination. Kernel wait4 peaks
remain per-process metadata, not a claim of a simultaneous aggregate peak.
Count both failed/current output directories against the original eight MiB.
Per-phase ceilings are 60 seconds preparation, 480 extraction, 120 comparison,
30 choice freeze, 90 evaluation, and the remaining closing reserve; total new
wall time must stay below 850. Preserve every unavailable phase or limit breach.
Generated scalar controls and the unchanged independent metric/feature audit
still precede publication. No raw pixels/Gram matrices, new media, passwords,
GPU, held-out ALASKA2 access or detector deployment follows.
