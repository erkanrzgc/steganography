## Fixed bounds and source policy

The new raw-transfer allowance is **512 MiB**, separate from the already
charged **55,307,394 bytes**. The incomplete historical total remains null.
Do not reset or recycle any old reservation, including partial failures.
Every request reserves its full declared extent plus one overflow byte before
network I/O. Retain immutable intent, prior-anchor, reservation and result
records; interrupted sources are consumed and cannot be retried under an
alias. Prior failed and completed originals are blocked by upstream SHA-1.

Use the unchanged `core.spatial_commons_native` service: original HTTPS
upload.wikimedia.org PNG files only, with no redirects or thumbnails; CC0
or CC BY 2.0/3.0/4.0 and version-specific attribution. A new source is at most
16 MiB, 16 million pixels, native 8-bit PNG L or RGB without alpha/palette,
single frame and ordinary orientation. No RGB-to-L conversion, resizing or
alpha removal. Expected hashes/extents, Commons revision and per-page
metadata evidence must be frozen in each reviewed batch before I/O.

Metadata discovery is bounded by eighty requests and 2 MiB per response;
metadata is not raw-media transfer or automatic admission. Each acquisition
batch has at most sixteen independently reviewed sources. Batch manifests
and source review sidecars are written exclusively; an attempt marker binds
their input digest before execution. The frozen driver delegates decoding
and acquisition to the existing shared service. Existing source code is not
modified. The previous full software gate and 100% admission-service coverage
remain the service QA baseline; driver-specific checks are reported separately.

The current scope is ordinary photographic imagery, including clearly
identified archival photographs. Drawings, engravings, charts, synthetic
renders, montages, unexplained sources and claimed licenses with conflicting
copyright notices are excluded from these batches. Any proposed microscopy
domain needs the user's separate preference recorded before its inclusion.
Published source/license claims are preserved without certifying pristine
capture history or absence of pre-existing hidden content.

Conservatively group each credited author's complete collection, preserve
known upstream package/recording/paper ancestry and merge known common
origins. Enforce a global maximum of eight admitted originals per group per
domain. A group represented in both modes remains one group. Do not assign
train/development/test roles or open existing reserved datasets.

## Quality checks and execution

The first pilot showed weak texture in two fixed crops. Before any new
embedding, require sample standard deviation at least **10** and pooled
256-bin sample entropy at least **5 bits** in the fixed top-left 256×256
crop. This rule was developed from the earlier unassigned pilot, not
preregistered before that pilot. Do not recrop or tune it after inspecting
new results. Preserve rejected originals and their charges. The four prior
pilot originals meeting this rule may be reused without a download; the two
flat examples remain historical pipeline fixtures.

Allow one attempt per newly selected original. Wait at least fifteen seconds
before each request. After HTTP 429, wait for subsequent distinct sources
according to the recorded Retry-After, with a minimum 120-second cooldown.
A Retry-After beyond 300 seconds stops the batch; never shorten a server's
longer requested pause. No failed-source retry, account switch or mirror
bypass is allowed. Use the parent `timeout` wrapper for a 1,800-second batch
ceiling, preserving all partial intent/reservation records on termination.

Target at most 128 accepted originals in each mode, including eligible
baseline originals. Exact duplicate crops are rejected. Record original
SHA-1/SHA-256, byte extent, depth/type, texture metrics and source group.
Native/texture success remains pending visual/ancestry review. Inspect full
image and fixed-crop contact sheets and pairwise similarity before final
corpus admission; heuristics cannot prove unrelated capture origins.

Prepare only reviewed completed originals. Keep the fixed crop before the
existing 32-byte generated LSB controls, preserve whole-origin groups across
PNG/BMP, and independently verify payloads, pixels and all output hashes.
Bound prepared media by **256 MiB**, keep it private on working disk, and
publish attribution and numerical inventories rather than image binaries.
Keep rejected/unavailable candidates visible in the results. Missing source
diversity or quantity stays missing, never training-ready by counting
derivatives, flat crops, drawings or converted RGB images as new L originals.

Training-loop implementation, generated learning control, optimizer/evaluation/
I/O profiling, executable recipe/source/data binding and actual fitting budget
remain separate later work. This collection changes no JPEG/WAV allowance and
does not relaunch the consumed 600-second capacity profile.
